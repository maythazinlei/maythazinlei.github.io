TRUTH SEEKER

May Thazin Lei is a Burmese documentary filmmaker and video journalist dedicated to uncovering complex political and social narratives across Southeast Asia. With a career built on frontline reporting and human rights storytelling, she currently produces documentary videos for BBC Burmese. Her foundational journalism career includes extensive work with leading independent networks, including DVB Multimedia Group and Mizzima Multimedia Group.
