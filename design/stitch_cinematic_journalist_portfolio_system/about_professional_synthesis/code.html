Philosophy

I bridge the gap between rigorous journalistic reporting and immersive documentary filmmaking.

My work is rooted in the belief that truth requires both factual precision and emotional resonance. By combining the technical scrutiny of investigative journalism with the visual language of cinema, I aim to amplify voices that are often relegated to the margins of history.

From the frontlines of political transition in Southeast Asia to the quiet nuances of human rights struggles, my lens seeks the structural tensions beneath the surface of the daily news cycle.
