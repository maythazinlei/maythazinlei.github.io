Profile

May Thazin Lei is an award-winning filmmaker and journalist based in Myanmar. With a focus on the shifting political landscapes and social undercurrents of Southeast Asia, her lens captures the raw reality of marginalized voices.

She has collaborated extensively with world-renowned media organizations including BBC Burmese, DVB Multimedia Group, and Mizzima Multimedia Group. Her work is characterized by a brutalist cinematic aesthetic that refuses to turn away from the difficult truths of our time.
