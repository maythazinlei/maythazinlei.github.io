SYNOPSIS

Deep in the forgotten frequencies of the Cold War, a mysterious signal begins to broadcast again after forty years of silence. "The Last Signal" follows a group of independent investigators as they trek across the frozen tundras of Siberia and the arid plains of the American Southwest to uncover the origin of a transmission that shouldn't exist.

Part investigative journalism, part cinematic meditation on the passage of time, this documentary explores the intersection of decaying technology and human obsession.

THE PROCESS

The production took place over 18 months, requiring a nimble two-person crew to navigate sensitive locations. Our visual approach was defined by long takes and naturalistic lighting, aiming to capture the 'haunted' quality of the signal's history without relying on traditional talking-head interviews.

"The truth isn't in what the signal says, but in why someone is still listening."
